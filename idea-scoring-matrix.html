Reading the output. Scores above 70 indicate strong alignment across desirability, viability, feasibility, strategic fit and novelty — these are candidates for prototyping. Scores of 45–69 warrant a second-pass discussion before committing resources. Scores below 45 should be parked or revisited when constraints change. The ranking reflects relative team judgment — a one-point difference is not meaningful; a band difference is.
