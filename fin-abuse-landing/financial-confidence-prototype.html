Three quiet next steps

None of these need to happen today, or in this order.

1

Think it through with someone you trust

Picture three people who know you well. If they watched a week of your financial life, what would they notice? You don’t have to call anyone — just notice what you already know.

2

Try one small thing, only when it’s safe to

Some people start with one financial decision made entirely alone: opening an account in their own name, keeping a small amount somewhere private, buying something just for themselves.

If a new account or a receipt would need explaining at home, it can wait. There’s no deadline here.

3

Talk to someone who does this every day

POWA supports people going through exactly this, confidentially and free of charge. Calling them doesn’t create a record with Nedbank, and nothing you say here follows you home.
