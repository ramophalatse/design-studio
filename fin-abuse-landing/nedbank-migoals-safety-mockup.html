Where we stand

We stand with survivors of financial abuse

Financial control is one of the most common, and least visible, forms of abuse. We built these features into MiGoals with people who have lived through it, so that no one has to choose between their safety and their bank account. They're free, permanent, and open to anyone who needs them.

A few more ways this account looks out for you

Everyday banking should feel safe. These features are built quietly into MiGoals for anyone who ever needs them.

Remove a cardholder, on your terms

If you’re the main accountholder, you’re always the one in control.

If someone else has a secondary card linked to your account, you can remove their access yourself, any time, in the app. There’s no explanation required, no waiting period, and no notification sent to them — it simply stops working.

Try it · Manage cardholders
JDJohn D. · Secondary card

Access removed. No alert was sent, and nothing appears in the transaction history.

Mute unwanted messages in payments

Anyone can attach a note to a payment. Now you can turn the notes off.

Some people use small payments to send messages you never asked for. Mute payment references from a specific account, or all of them, in a couple of taps. You’ll still see every amount that comes in or out — just not the words attached to it.

A quiet way to reach real support

Help that doesn’t look like a helpline.

Support is tucked into the app like any other feature, so opening it doesn’t look unusual to anyone who might be looking over your shoulder. On the other end is a real person trained to help, on your terms, whenever you’re ready.

Emergency funds when you need them most

Grant funding for people facing a genuine crisis, at no cost to you.

If life takes a sudden, difficult turn, you may qualify for emergency grant funding through one of our NGO partners. We verify your circumstances quickly and handle every case with care and discretion.

1Tell us what’s going on — in the app, or with someone on the quiet helpline above.
2We verify with a partner NGO — quickly, and in confidence.
3Funds land in your MiGoals account — ready to use straight away.
