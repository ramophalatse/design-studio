01
Discovery: lead with the incentive

The offer surfaces before any commitment is asked for, it's part of the entry proposition, not a post-signup reward. Quantifying the value as "up to R240 in savings" makes the offer tangible and comparable to real spend.

Design intent: Two CTAs: Primary "open account" and secondary "learn more first" — accommodate both the ready-to-go user and the one who needs more context before committing.
02
Consideration: Unpack what the offer actually means

Existing web users or newcomers may not understand what "live pricing" means in practice. This screen earns the click by making the value concrete: real-time vs 15-minute delayed data, and a standard monthly cost of R80.

The unlock conditions are explicit and deliberately low-friction. Any deposit qualifies, removing the hesitation around minimum amounts.

Open question: Should a minimum deposit threshold apply to unlock the offer (e.g. R500 or R1 000)? This changes the copy and drop-off risk at the funding step.
03
Funding: The reward banner persists at the point of commitment

The reward is reinforced on the deposit screen, the moment of highest drop-off risk. The user is reminded exactly what activates on confirmation, reducing hesitation.

The CTA copy shifts from a generic "confirm" to "confirm deposit + unlock offer" — making the reward feel like the primary outcome of the action, not a side effect.

Design intent: The "any amount unlocks" nudge is a deliberate low-friction signal, it tells the user they don't need to commit a large sum to get the full benefit, reducing the psychological barrier to first deposit.
04
Activation: A celebration moment with built-in retention mechanics

The confirmation screen is a delight moment, not just a receipt. The 90-day countdown creates mild urgency to use live pricing before it expires, naturally driving ongoing app engagement during the trial window.

The badge and watchlist nudge anchor the next action: the user has live pricing, the nudge tells them immediately how to use it.

Design intent: The 90-day counter is an assumption based on 3 months. Confirm the exact validity period and whether it resets on any condition (e.g. early upgrade to paid live pricing).
Open question: What happens at day 91? A well-timed reminder at day 75 ("15 days of live pricing left... here's what you'd pay to keep it") could convert free users to paid before expiry.
